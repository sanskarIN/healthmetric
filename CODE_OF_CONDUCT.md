# HealthMetric Code of Conduct

## Our standard

HealthMetric contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to communicate respectfully, focus on the work, and make participation welcoming to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Examples of constructive behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- assuming good faith while still addressing mistakes clearly;
- respecting privacy and avoiding requests for personal health information;
- using neutral language when discussing body measurements and health references;
- accepting corrections and maintaining evidence-based documentation.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, repeated personal attacks, deliberate sharing of another person's private information, or using health/body measurements to shame or rank people.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, project-managed community spaces, and direct interactions undertaken as a representative of HealthMetric.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `sanskarin@outlook.in`. Include enough context for maintainers to investigate, but do not send unnecessary personal health information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or restrict participation when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and documented privately where appropriate.

## Good-faith technical disagreement

Disagreement about architecture, evidence, accessibility, privacy, or implementation is welcome when it remains specific and respectful. Critique the change, not the person.
